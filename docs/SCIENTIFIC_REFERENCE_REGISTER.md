# Scientific reference register — dromedary camel

This register identifies literature that can guide review and data acquisition. A citation is not permission to copy figures, scans, meshes, or supplementary files. Check each publication's license and obtain source data from the authors/institution where needed.

| Region / workflow | Reference | Use in this project | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| General anatomy | CVRL, *Anatomical Atlas of the Dromedary Camel* | Primary specialist atlas to consult for terminology and gross anatomy | Commercial publication; obtain lawful access. |
| Head / brain / skull | Kabkia et al. (2026), *Virtual Anatomical Sections of the Dromedary Head*, DOI: 10.1155/vmi/3214441 | CT-based head topography and virtual sections | One 18-month male specimen; not a population atlas. |
| Distal limb / pes | Hamoda et al. (2025), *Imaging-based observation of the pes region...*, DOI: 10.3389/fvets.2025.1558954 | CT, MRI and ultrasound references for bones, tendons and ligaments | Study used 16 pes regions from adult males; sex/age coverage is limited. |

## Scientific use policy
All catalogue entries remain unverified until a qualified reviewer checks dromedary-specific evidence. A study on a single male or a small regional sample must not be generalized to all ages, sexes, breeds, or body regions. Do not infer absent structures or surgical safe zones from incomplete imaging.
