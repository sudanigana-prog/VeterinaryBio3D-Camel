# Anatomy review checklist

For each structure, confirm:

- Dromedary camel (*Camelus dromedarius*) source, not inferred from another species.
- Sex applicability and age/physiological context where relevant.
- Specimen/source identifier and imaging or dissection modality.
- Anatomical naming cross-checked against a reliable camel reference.
- Mesh orientation, scale, segmentation boundaries, and topology reviewed.
- Neurovascular relationships recorded where surgically relevant.
- Source license/permission recorded.
- Reviewer identity/role and review date recorded.
- Verification state updated only after review.

Surgical training approval requires additional procedure-specific evidence and expert sign-off; it must not be inferred from anatomical verification alone.
